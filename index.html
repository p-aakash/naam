            Before you file

              How Naam works

              Live

              Domain availability is looked up directly from each registry's public RDAP server, falling back to DNS-over-HTTPS for extensions without RDAP.

              Guided

              State registries, the USPTO and social networks don't offer open lookups to websites, so Naam checks their rules and gives you one-click links to the official searches.

              Private

              No accounts, no tracking, no server. Your name idea only leaves your browser as domain lookups sent to registries and DNS resolvers.

              Naam is an informational tool, not legal advice. Rules vary by state and change over time. Confirm with the official registry, and consider an attorney for trademark clearance.
